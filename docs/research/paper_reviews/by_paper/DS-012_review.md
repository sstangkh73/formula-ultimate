## Research question

Can ex-ante technological radicalness be separated into new functionality and new technological/scientific origins using patent data?

## Method and evidence

The study operationalizes novelty with patent classifications and citation information, then validates indicators on a large patent-application sample using breakthrough outcomes and controls (pp. 2, 11-12).

## Principal findings

- **Paper finding (conservative paraphrase):** New functionality and new origins are correlated but convey distinct information; correlations among separate dimension indicators are generally no more than 0.3, and the IPC4-level indicators show positive significant relationships with breakthrough probability (pp. 2, 11-12).
- **Reviewer interpretation:** The paper shows why one novelty score is inadequate: a design can use unfamiliar knowledge without adding function, or add function from familiar origins.
- **Confidence:** moderate for identifying the transferable idea; no independent replication was performed.

## Limitations and transfer boundary

Patent indicators depend on classification/citation practice, corpus, time, and the absence of consensus on radical innovation (p. 3).
