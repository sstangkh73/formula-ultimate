# Work 103 Plan: Generation-First, Physics-Evaluation Roadmap

Thai companion: `2026-09-05_103_generation-first-physics-evaluation-roadmap-plan.th.md`

Status: Completed

## Objective

Document the present Formula Ultimate generation/evaluation architecture and replace its implicit representation-first and premature-gating assumptions with a detailed generation-first plan: candidates may express unfamiliar morphology first, while physics measures, explains, and ranks what was created. Preserve hard anti-exploit, finite-resource, evidence, and promotion boundaries without prescribing conventional part shapes.

## Scope and planned files

- `docs/reports/GENERATION_FIRST_PHYSICS_EVALUATION_ROADMAP_V1.md` and Thai companion.
- this plan/result record and Thai companions.

This is an append-oriented architecture and experiment plan. Older roadmaps and historical results remain unchanged so the prior state is not rewritten as if the new direction had always been implemented.

## Planned content

1. Record the before-state from Work 090 and Works 091–097: primitive scalar search, frozen free-form corpora, typed graph-only mutation, synthetic pre-performance manufacturing fixtures, fixed semantic selectors, and seven frozen reduced-order structural adapters.
2. Separate constraints into representation invariants, external task/resource constraints, measurable physics outcomes, manufacturability outcomes, numerical/evidence states, and promotion-only requirements.
3. Define a new candidate lifecycle from open-ended genotype and executable geometry generation through measurement, boundary-condition binding, solver attempts, continuous outcome records, quality-diversity archiving, and fidelity promotion.
4. Specify the new genome/operator capability needed for control-point, field, topology, interface, material-distribution, and mechanism/controller co-evolution rather than replacement from a frozen solid library.
5. Define how physically failed, manufacturing-incompatible, and numerically unresolved candidates remain observable evidence instead of being conflated or silently deleted.
6. Map revisions into detailed implementation works with independent/dependent variables, controls, metrics, success/failure criteria, migration risks, and explicit non-goals.

## Validation and success criteria

- Every current-state claim must point to a repository source, retained artifact, or completed report and preserve its original claim boundary.
- The new flow must never admit NaN/infinite resources, hidden energy, hidden repair, missing provenance, solver exploitation, or unsupported physical claims.
- Geometry novelty may not compensate for physical failure, but physical/manufacturing failure may be retained as a measured search outcome and stepping-stone archive entry.
- `numerically_unresolved`, `physically_failed`, `manufacturing_incompatible`, and `representation_invalid` must be separate states.
- The roadmap must identify executable changes needed before Work 100, rather than claiming current Work 097 already evaluates arbitrary geometry.
- English/Thai companions, local links, terminology, identifiers, numbers, and limitations must remain aligned.
- Repository-contract tests and Markdown diff checks must pass before a scoped commit.

## Risks and non-goals

Moving gates later can waste compute or invite simulator exploits unless budgets, escalation rules, conservation audits, and invalid-state accounting remain explicit. General solid/implicit representations can be difficult to mesh; inability to solve is not proof of physical failure. Manufacturing cannot become optional for final promotion, only non-destructive during early exploration.

No CAD generator, physics solver, search algorithm, configuration, experiment, external publication, or existing roadmap is changed in Work 103. This document does not validate a vehicle or guarantee that open-ended search will discover a useful mechanism.
