## Objective

Define an implementation-ready roadmap that expands Formula Ultimate from fixed primitive scaling and hand-authored candidate builders into topology-mutating, free-form, geometry-causal design search capable of proposing genuinely unfamiliar functional parts.

The roadmap must distinguish visual curvature from real design freedom. It must expand representation, mutation, semantic interfaces, geometry inspection, meshing, physical evaluation, and quality-diversity search together while preserving deterministic replay, SI units, evidence gates, fair baselines, and the prohibition on hidden result-conditioned geometry repair.

## Validation and success criteria

- The roadmap must cover search-space measurement, free-form profiles and solids, topology genomes, typed mutations, deterministic constructive repair, semantic geometry witnesses, generalized meshing/physics, quality-diversity search, isolated subsystem trials, integrated candidates, and higher-fidelity promotion.
- Every future work item must have a falsifiable success criterion and identify what it does not prove.
- The plan must not prescribe conventional vehicle forms or treat novelty of appearance as functional discovery.
- English/Thai structure, identifiers, work numbers, equations, units, gates, and limitations must match.
- Repository-contract tests and Markdown companion checks must pass.

## Risks and explicit non-goals

Risks include adding curved operators without connecting them to search, rewarding visual oddity, letting complex candidates consume unfair compute, hiding invalid geometry through repair, and creating a grammar that physics cannot evaluate. This planning work does not implement CAD operators, run an evolution campaign, generate a new candidate, or claim that open-ended discovery has been achieved.
