## Decisions and deviations

- Gmsh's exact ASCII MSH 2.2 mesh is converted into a project-owned CalculiX
  C3D4 deck; no mesh is regenerated by the solver adapter.
- Loaded-face nodal forces use surface-triangle tributary area and close to the
  declared `1000 N` resultant.
- Mean `Sxx` is tetrahedron-volume weighted. Stress output must cover the exact
  tetrahedral element set, and total mesh volume must close to declared volume.
- Parser tables are bounded by named headers and reject missing, non-finite, or
  duplicate evidence identities. This was added after a first failed run showed
  that an unbounded stress block also admitted strain rows and produced a false
  approximately `5 MPa` mean.
- Solver-reported internal strain energy was not parsed. The energy gate uses
  load-weighted external work `0.5*sum(F_i*u_i)` against `F*delta/2`; this is
  explicitly a limitation rather than independent energy agreement.
- The artifact summary retains process stdout, including Gmsh mesh-quality
  diagnostics, rather than translating that text into an undocumented scalar.

## Falsification review

Supporting evidence: three fresh connected meshes, exact finite-volume/load
coverage, closed reactions, analytical agreement, and convergence. No accepted
run contradicted the preferred local linear-tension hypothesis after the parser
defect was corrected and tests fixed the table boundary.

Alternative explanations remain: the uniform prismatic specimen is unusually
simple and analytical agreement does not generalize to arbitrary geometry or
nonlinear failure. Confidence is high only for this local installed solver
route.

## Limitations and follow-up

Independent-solver agreement and physical coupon data are absent. Bending,
torsion, buckling, loaded interfaces, yield/plasticity, fracture, fatigue,
connection removal, subsystem failure, and vehicle `DNF` remain unimplemented.
The next separately planned milestone should add the beam-bending specimen and
an independently derived deflection/stress gate before whole-vehicle work.
