# Optimized Vehicle Controls V1

Thai companion: `OPTIMIZED_VEHICLE_CONTROLS_V1.th.md`

Status: Implemented by Work 127 as a registered fairness and substitution gate.

## Evidence boundary

Fixed-topology, reference, random-control and open-candidate arms receive the same external task, component-library opportunity, source energy, safety boundary, paired conditions and separate vehicle-search/controller-tuning budgets. Every arm is optimized. The fixed topology is a control only and does not constrain the open arm's layout.

Common-controller substitution is evaluated before matched-budget retuning. The system estimand is the retuned open candidate minus the best retuned non-open control. Installed base, cooling, containment and support mass, source/use energy and manufacturing penalty are transferred into every score. An untuned baseline, zero-cost controller adaptation, incomplete burden ledger or unequal source energy invalidates fairness.

A system-benefit claim requires the lower registered paired interval to reach the meaningful-effect threshold without failures or hidden burden. Exact replay requires the same result SHA-256. The response fixtures are synthetic, and Work 126 remains exploratory; this contract does not establish held-out race performance, external novelty, promotion or physical validation.
