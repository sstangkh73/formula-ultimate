## Evidence boundary

The fixed-family, existing-grammar, open-material and random-control arms receive the same task, synthetic material/process boundary, parameter count, optimization evaluations and paired holdout conditions. Training and holdout identifiers must be disjoint. Every arm is optimized before comparison; an untuned control is not an admissible baseline.

The registered estimand is open-material minus the best optimized non-open control at fine fidelity. A benefit claim requires complete constraints and a lower paired interval at or above the preregistered meaningful effect. Omitted hardware invalidates an evaluation. Rejected and unresolved geometries remain subject to score-independent audit, and a claimed active coupling must lose its effect under targeted ablation while an inactive appendage remains unchanged.

The deterministic fixtures are synthetic. A coarse positive ranking that reverses at fine fidelity is contradicting evidence and blocks a benefit claim. Exact replay requires the same result SHA-256. This contract does not establish external novelty, measured material survival, whole-vehicle benefit, manufacturing feasibility, or physical validation.
